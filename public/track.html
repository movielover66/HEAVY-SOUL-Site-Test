When will my order arrive?

Your estimated delivery date is shown at the top of your order. It refreshes as the courier scans your parcel, and on the delivery day you will see it as "Out for delivery".

The status has not changed. Is something wrong?

Courier scans can pause for a day, especially during long road legs between hubs. This page refreshes every 30 seconds, so just keep it open. If nothing moves for 2 days, message us.

Can I cancel or change my order?

If cancelling is still possible, a Cancel button appears on your order with a countdown. For an address or size change, message us on WhatsApp as early as you can.

How does Cash on Delivery work?

You pay a small advance online and the balance to the rider at your door. The exact balance is shown in your order summary and again on the delivery day.

How do returns and exchanges work?

Read the full steps on our policies page, or message us with your Order ID and we will guide you.

I cannot find my Order ID.

It was sent to you on WhatsApp right after checkout. You can also open My orders after logging in.
